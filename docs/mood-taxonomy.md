# Bananasutra Mood Taxonomy

One mood per track. Mood captures the musical character and emotional feel — distinct from tempo, intention, sutra, and topic tags.

---

## CHEEKY
Witty, ironic, sarcastic. The knowing smirk, the double meaning, the provocation disguised as charm. Sharp humor with a wink.

## GROOVY
Rhythmic, cool, funky. The head-nod, the swagger, the pocket. You feel it in your body before your brain catches up.

## HOLY
Spiritual, transcendent, sacred. Music that reaches upward — devotion, awe, reverence, the feeling of touching something bigger.

## KINDLY
Warm, gentle, heart-centered. Tenderness with purpose. The musical expression of kindness as a core value — soft but never weak.

## NERDY
Heady, cerebral, intricate. Music for the mind — layered, clever, rewarding close attention. The joy of complexity.

## PEACHY
Sensual, kinky, body-forward. Flowsutra territory. Music that lives in the skin, the breath, the heat. Unapologetically physical.

## PUNKY
Rebellious, defiant, WTF. The raised fist, the side-eye, the refusal to play nice. Attitude first, apology never.

## RAINY
Melancholic, nostalgic, bittersweet. The ache that isn't sharp — it's slow, it lingers, it stares out windows. Beautiful sadness.

## SILLY
Playful, goofy, lighthearted. Music that doesn't take itself seriously and doesn't want you to either. Permission to be absurd.

## TRIPPY
Weird, psychedelic, disorienting. Music that bends perception — the sonic equivalent of "wait, what just happened?" Reality optional.

---

*Ten moods. No overlap. No manual needed.*
